Description

The JBL Boombox 4 is built for the moments that need big sound. Dual woofers and tweeters deliver deep, punchy bass and crisp highs that fill any room, backyard or beach without distortion, even at full volume.

With up to 34 hours of playtime and a built-in power bank, you stay in the mix all day and keep your phone charged. The rugged IP67 design shrugs off water, sand and dust, and the sturdy handle makes it easy to carry the energy everywhere you go.

Pair two speakers for stereo sound, or connect more with the JBL app and turn any gathering into a party.
